Our Mission

Empowering Every Nepali Student Through Free, High-Yield Exam Preparation

अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्। तयारी गर्नुहोस्। सफल हुनुहोस्। (Practice. Prepare. Pass.)

ExamSetu Nepal was founded with a singular purpose: to bridge the digital and educational divide for students and job aspirants across Nepal. Whether you reside in the Kathmandu Valley or a remote district in Karnali or Sudurpashchim Province, access to high-quality MCQ model sets, instant feedback, and verified explanations should never depend on expensive coaching institutes.

What "ExamSetu" Means

In Nepali and Sanskrit, "Setu" (सेतु) means a bridge. ExamSetu stands as the bridge between your dedicated study effort and passing your dream competitive examination. We combine rigorous academic accuracy with modern, lightweight web technology to create an exam simulator that runs smoothly on any smartphone.

Key Pillars of ExamSetu

Rigorous Accuracy

All questions are thoroughly researched and verified against official constitutions, laws, and board textbooks.

Authentic Simulation

Realistic timers, question palettes, and review modes train you to maintain composure under strict time limits.

Independent Educational Platform

ExamSetu Nepal is an independent educational initiative. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Public Service Commission (Lok Sewa Aayog), the National Examination Board (NEB), or any government ministry. Our practice sets are developed solely for educational and self-assessment purposes.
