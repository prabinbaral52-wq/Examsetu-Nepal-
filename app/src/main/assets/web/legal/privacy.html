Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

At ExamSetu Nepal (accessible from examsetu.np), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by ExamSetu Nepal and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, participate in mock exams, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Account details such as your name and email address.
  • Test performance metrics (scores, time taken, answered questions).
  • Browser and device logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps).

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

ExamSetu Nepal uses cookies to improve user experience and deliver relevant advertisements:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads).

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard cryptographic techniques, including bcrypt password hashing, parameterized database queries, and secure session management to safeguard user data. We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal identification information to outside parties.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@examsetu.np.
